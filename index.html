      Hey, I’m Soline.

      From care planning to cybersecurity to 3D for architects, I turn dense software into something people understand and own.

      I’m insatiably curious. Complex subjects keep me on my feet: I need to understand, so I’m always investigating something, somewhere, for someone. My sweet spot is making that complexity easy to grasp, with clear workflows and UI patterns, or with diagrams and analogies. And I love running workshops with my peers: sketching together often gets a team further than another meeting.

      I believe in wonderment. Putting as much of it as I can into everything I make is my core mission as a designer.
